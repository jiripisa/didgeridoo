Didgeridoo
Manual Download GitHub Česky

A playable synthesized didgeridoo: drone, moving overtones, voice, breath and rhythm, as an Audio Unit and a standalone app.

Download for macOS Version 1.0.2 for Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later. Read the manual.

What it does

A drone you can shape

Lips that shut for half of every cycle, a tongue that carves the moving overtone out of the sound, a tube with its own resonances, breath, and a voice sung into the tube: the instrument as acoustics research measured it. Hold a key for the drone; play another and the pitch slides there.

Shape it while you play

The mod wheel raises the tongue and moves the overtone, aftertouch brings in the sung voice, the pitch wheel leans on the note, and a harder hit is a louder drone. Two LFOs move any of sixteen knobs for you, each by as much as you say.

Rhythm that follows the tempo

A pulse ducks the volume and pushes the vowel on every eighth, sixteenth or any other division, with accents and quick in-breaths, locked to your host's song.

Plug-in and standalone app

One download gives you the app, with its own MIDI input and audio output settings, and an Audio Unit for Ableton Live, Logic Pro and other hosts. Ten programs are built in, four of them shaped after measurements of real instruments.

Download

The disk image is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without security warnings. Drag Didgeridoo to Applications. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later.

Build from source

You need the Xcode command line tools; no Xcode project is involved.

cd didgeridoo
./run.sh
